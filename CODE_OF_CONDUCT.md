# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We welcome contributors with different languages, teaching contexts, experience levels, and accessibility needs. Participate with respect, give specific evidence-based feedback, and discuss the work without attacking the person.

Acceptable behavior includes:

- explaining language judgments with grammar, usage, meaning, or learning evidence;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- protecting student, teacher, and source-document privacy;
- making review accessible and constructive.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate humiliation, sexualized conduct, doxxing, publishing private learning data, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate this standard. Serious or repeated violations may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub profile. Include relevant links or screenshots while minimizing personal data. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
